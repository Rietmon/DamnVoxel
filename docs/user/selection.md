# Selecting and moving

## Select
- With a rectangle (**M**), a lasso (**K**), a box in space (**U**), the magic wand (**W**) — by volume, by face or by color (**Alt+W**).
- Shift — add, Alt — subtract.
- **Ctrl+A** — all, **Ctrl+D** — none, **Ctrl+I** — invert, **Ctrl+]** / **Ctrl+[** — grow / shrink.
- **Alt+C** — select the voxels of the colors chosen in the palette; the other way — “colors from the selection” in the palette.

## Edit only the selection
While there is a selection, brushes and fills do not go outside it; the “outside the selection” mode protects the selection from edits.

## Move (T)
The selection becomes a floating piece: drag it, turn it by 90° and by any angle, flip it, scale it. Enter or a click elsewhere drops it. More: repeat the piece as an array, loop it (shift around inside the box), crop the object to the box with an anchor.

## Copy
**Ctrl+C / Ctrl+X / Ctrl+V** — between documents too (colors are matched to the palette). **Ctrl+J** — duplicate the selection, **Delete** — delete it.

## Sets and extracting
- **Ctrl+Alt+S** remembers the selection under a name — bring it back from the list of sets.
- **Ctrl+Shift+E** moves the selection into a new object (or onto a new layer).
