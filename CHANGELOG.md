# Changelog

## Unreleased — 1.0.0, the first public release

### Editing
- Brush with volume, plane and surface modes, cube or ball, pen pressure, axis and plane locks.
- Face extrude and push, shapes (box, line, rectangle, ellipse, sphere, cylinder, cone, pyramid, torus, stairs, arch), smooth SDF shapes, Bézier curves and splines, stamps, sculpting (grow, erode, smooth, bumps, flatten), fills, hollowing and removing hidden voxels.
- A live mirror on any combination of axes, slicing, “adjust last operation”, unlimited undo for everything.
- Selection by rectangle, lasso, box and magic wand; moving with turns by any angle, flips and scaling; arrays, loop, crop; named selection sets; editing only inside or outside the selection.

### Scene and color
- Objects, groups, layers, linked copies, booleans, aligning and putting on the ground, an asset library, reference pictures.
- Ropes, chains and cables between two points, cloth draped over objects.
- A 256-color palette with ramps, usage highlighting, names, row notes, a palette library and palette files in and out; painting with gradients, noise and splatter, tone shifts, color lock; MagicaVoxel materials.

### Animation and render
- Model frames and position keys on a timeline, smooth motion, baking, onion skin — compatible with `.vox`.
- Path tracing with materials, sky, HDR panoramas, fog, depth of field, bloom and denoising; a render window over editing; frames up to 8192 px, turntables and animations as GIF or video, isometric sprite sheets.

### Programs
- Shapes from JavaScript or from a node graph, with live parameters, stored inside `.vox`.

### Files
- Lossless `.vox` reading and writing, tabs, autosave, version history, recovery of damaged files.
- Export to glTF / GLB, OBJ, PLY and STL; voxelizing glTF / OBJ / STL; pictures, height maps and slice sheets to voxels; PNG slices out.

### Interface
- Desktop and phone layouts, mouse, touchpad, finger and pen; MagicaVoxel, Blockbench and Blender key layouts with remapping; a command palette, a radial menu, number fields with expressions, light and dark themes; installs as a PWA and works offline.
